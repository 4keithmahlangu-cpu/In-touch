THE STORY PLAY
The story

Raised by their grandmother, two sisters shared one favourite game: tag. When she is gone, the game stops and the sisters grow apart. Years later they meet again in an imaginary space where time is physically visible, a six-metre clock painted in primary colour, and they wish they could turn it back. Their younger selves and their grandmother return as memory, and the only rules the sisters still share are the rules of the game. One seeks, one is sought, and every touch pulls them closer or pushes them apart. They wear the board on their bodies: the younger carries childhood in one yellow pocket, the older carries everything after.

The game in the theatre

Every seat comes with a card: Seeker or Sought. Play your part in their story, and when the clock stops, make the call. Are they still in touch?

Influences
Constructivism. Popova (1922; Worrall, 1973): the stage, and the dungaree, as machines for acting.
De Stijl. Mondrian (1917); Van Doesburg (1927): primary colour as territory you can step into.
Indigenous games. Ferreira (2014); Mathunjwa et al. (2025): tag as living heritage, chasers and runners in a defined space.
Play. Huizinga (1949): the clock as the magic circle where the rules hold.
The spectator. Rancière (2009); Boal (1979): the audience as game master.

HOW THE GAME TELLS THE STORY
1

Twelve hours, one lifetime

Each level is an hour on the six-metre clock from In-Touch. The sisters grow from little girls at 2 o’clock to old women at 1.

2

Run, then play

Run to each hour’s gate, past grief, harsh words and years of silence. At the gate, Gogo sets a challenge built on that hour’s set piece: diketo in her yard, kgati with the twisted ropes, a wall of harsh words to break.

3

Bond, memories, stars

Touch your sister and choose kindly to grow your Bond. Spend memories in Gogo’s tin. Earn up to three stars on every level.

4

The ending is yours

Reach home base at 1 o’clock with a Bond of 15 or more to end in touch. Then you make the final call, as the audience does in the theatre.
